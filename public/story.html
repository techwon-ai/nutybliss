Our Journey

How we got here.

💡
EARLY 2024
The "Why can't I read the label?" moment

Ahmed picks up a peanut butter jar at the supermarket. Reads the label. Palm oil. Emulsifiers. Maltodextrin. Modified starch. Puts it back. Decides to make his own.

🔧
MID 2024
The garage grinder era

Ahmed and Maryam (his sister) spent six weekends perfecting the stone-grind method. 47 test batches. More peanuts consumed than anyone should admit. Batch #47 was the one.

🚀
SEPT 2024
First 100 jars — sold in 4 hours

We listed 100 jars of Classic Smooth on Instagram. Sold out in 4 hours. Our DMs exploded. We knew we had something real.

🏭
DEC 2024
Real facility. Same recipe.

Moved to a PSQCA-certified facility in Lahore. Scaled production. Did NOT change the recipe. The garage formula lives on — just made at scale, with the same stone grinders.

🌈
2025
5 flavors, 50,000 jars, 0 regrets

Launched Crunchy, Choco Bliss, Choc-Chip, Honey Drip, and Protein+. Hit 50K jars sold. Still using the same peanuts. Still reading every single review.

What We Stand For

The NUTYBLISS promise.

🚫
Zero Junk

No palm oil. No added sugar. No emulsifiers. No stabilizers. No hydrogenated fats. If we can't pronounce it, it's not in the jar.

🌿
Real Ingredients

Just peanuts. Sometimes a pinch of salt. Our Choco has just cocoa. Our Honey has just honey. Every ingredient is intentional.

🔬
Honest Nutrition

25g of protein per 100g. Healthy fats. No sugar crash. No inflated numbers. What's on the label is what's in the jar.

🌱
Responsible Sourcing

Non-GMO peanuts from select farms. Minimal packaging. No single-use plastic. Because the planet matters as much as protein.

💬
Radical Transparency

Full ingredient list. Full nutrition facts. No hiding behind "natural flavors." You deserve to know exactly what you're eating.

❤️
Made with Love

Every batch is still checked by Ahmed or Maryam personally. Quality isn't a department — it's a mindset we built into every jar.
